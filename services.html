03

How an order runs.

Example · single jersey from stock

  1. 01 You

    Send the brief

    Tech pack, sample or sketch, with quantities, target date and destination.

  2. 02 You + us

    Sample + approve

    We develop and sample in-house. You approve fit, fabric and artwork.

  3. 03 Us

    Produce

    Fabric from stock or via our China office. Cut, print or embroider, sew.

  4. 04 Us

    Inspect + pack

    In-line checks and a final AQL inspection, then packing to your spec.

  5. 05 Us

    Ship

    Green Point Logistics handles customs and freight. Duty-free into the US, EU and GCC.
