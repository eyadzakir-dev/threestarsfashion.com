Full-service manufacturing, from development to delivery.

Cutting, sewing, printing, embroidery, finishing, quality assurance and product development, run by our own teams in the Alexandria Free Zone.

01

Seven capabilities. One group.

Service sheet

TSF offers a complete range of garment manufacturing services. Our integrated facilities and experienced production teams ensure consistent output at high volumes while maintaining international quality and compliance standards.

  1. 01

    Precision cutting

    Our cutting department uses advanced CAD/CAM technology for precise, efficient cutting of fabrics. Every pattern is optimized for minimal waste while maintaining the highest quality standards.

    • Computer-aided design integration
    • Automated cutting systems
    • Pattern optimization
    • Multi-layer cutting capability
    • Quality inspection at every stage

  2. 02

    Expert sewing

    Our sewing operations run 3,100+ machines across multiple factory buildings, staffed by trained operators delivering consistent quality across activewear, casualwear, uniforms, and underwear.

    • Modern sewing machinery
    • Trained production operators
    • Multiple product lines
    • Strict quality control
    • High-volume capacity

  3. 03

    Screen printing

    With 6 advanced printing machines and a daily capacity of 65,000+ pieces, our printing facility delivers vibrant, durable prints that withstand repeated washing and wear.

    • 6 high-capacity machines
    • 65,000+ pieces daily capacity
    • Multi-color printing
    • Water-based and plastisol inks
    • Special effects available

  4. 04

    Embroidery

    Our computerized embroidery department creates intricate designs with precision and consistency. From logos to complex patterns, we deliver professional results every time.

    • Computerized embroidery machines
    • Multi-head capability
    • Custom design digitization
    • Wide thread color selection
    • Consistent quality output

  5. 05

    Packing & finishing

    The final stage ensures every garment is finished, pressed, and packaged according to client specifications. We handle every aspect of preparation for shipping.

    • Professional pressing
    • Quality final inspection
    • Custom packaging options
    • Labeling and tagging
    • Export-ready preparation

  6. 06

    Quality assurance

    Our QA process ensures every garment meets international standards, with strict protocols maintained throughout the entire production chain.

    • In-line quality checks
    • Final inspection protocols
    • AQL sampling standards
    • Customer specification compliance
    • Continuous improvement processes

  7. 07

    Product development & sourcing

    From initial concept to bulk production, our in-house design and development team supports customers with sampling, customization, and global fabric sourcing.

    • In-house design and sampling
    • Dedicated sourcing office in China
    • 450+ tons cotton/CVC jersey stock
    • Performance & sustainable fabrics
    • Custom specification development
