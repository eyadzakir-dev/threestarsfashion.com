COMPREHENSIVE SOLUTIONS

From Development to Delivery

TSF offers a complete range of garment manufacturing services across cutting, sewing, printing, embroidery, finishing, quality assurance, and product development. Our integrated facilities and experienced production teams ensure consistent output at high volumes while maintaining international quality and compliance standards.

01

Precision Cutting

Our cutting department utilizes advanced CAD/CAM technology to ensure precise, efficient cutting of fabrics. Every pattern is optimized for minimal waste while maintaining the highest quality standards.

  • Computer-aided design integration
  • Automated cutting systems
  • Pattern optimization
  • Multi-layer cutting capability
  • Quality inspection at every stage
02

Expert Sewing

Our sewing operations run 3,100+ machines across multiple factory buildings, staffed by trained operators delivering consistent quality across activewear, casualwear, uniforms, and underwear categories.

  • Modern sewing machinery
  • Trained production operators
  • Multiple product lines
  • Strict quality control
  • High-volume capacity
03

Screen Printing

With 6 advanced printing machines and a daily capacity of 65,000+ pieces, our printing facility delivers vibrant, durable prints that withstand repeated washing and wear.

  • 6 high-capacity machines
  • 65,000+ pieces daily capacity
  • Multi-color printing
  • Water-based and plastisol inks
  • Special effects available
04

Embroidery

Our computerized embroidery department creates intricate designs with precision and consistency. From logos to complex patterns, we deliver professional results every time.

  • Computerized embroidery machines
  • Multi-head capability
  • Custom design digitization
  • Wide thread color selection
  • Consistent quality output
05

Packing & Finishing

The final stage ensures every garment is perfectly finished, pressed, and packaged according to client specifications. We handle all aspects of preparation for shipping.

  • Professional pressing
  • Quality final inspection
  • Custom packaging options
  • Labeling and tagging
  • Export-ready preparation
06

Quality Assurance

Our comprehensive QA process ensures that every garment meets international standards. We maintain strict protocols throughout the entire production chain.

  • In-line quality checks
  • Final inspection protocols
  • AQL sampling standards
  • Customer specification compliance
  • Continuous improvement processes
07

Product Development & Sourcing

From initial concept to bulk production, our in-house design and development team supports customers with comprehensive sampling, customization, and global fabric sourcing solutions.

  • In-house design and sampling
  • Dedicated sourcing office in China
  • 450+ tons cotton/CVC jersey stock
  • Performance & sustainable fabrics
  • Custom specification development

Need a Tailored Manufacturing Solution?

We work with brands and sourcing teams to develop production solutions tailored to specific program requirements, volumes, and compliance needs.
