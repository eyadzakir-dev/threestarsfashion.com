Overview

The Garment Production Manager manages day-to-day manufacturing execution across our active production lines. You will drive line balancing, capacity utilization, labor efficiency, and daily takt pacing to meet delivery commitments without compromising safety or garment construction fidelity.

01Key deliverables

  • D/01Orchestrate daily operations across multi-line sewing layouts, coordinating continuous bundle feeding from cutting to sewing and finishing.
  • D/02Monitor hourly output versus daily targets, maximizing line efficiency based on Standard Allowed Minutes (SAM/SMV) and machine allocations.
  • D/03Reduce changeover downtime and learning-curve decay during style switches through structured line balancing and mechanical setups.
  • D/04Partner with industrial engineers and floor maintenance leads to clear bottlenecks, mitigate operator downtime, and maintain safety standards.

02About Three Stars Group

One of Egypt’s largest apparel manufacturers: 10 production units, 3,500 sewing machines and 100,000 units a day for brands across the US and Europe, all in Alexandria, Egypt.
