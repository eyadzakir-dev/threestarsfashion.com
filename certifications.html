Certified for quality, compliance and sustainability.

TSF maintains a portfolio of international certifications and buyer approvals covering quality management, ethical labor practices, environmental sustainability, and product safety. They are independently audited and represent verified operational standards, not marketing claims.

01

Our standards.

Compliance register

  • No. 01

    ISO 9001Quality management

    Quality management system certification ensuring consistent product quality, customer satisfaction, and continuous improvement across all processes.

  • No. 02

    WRAPGoldResponsible production

    Gold-level Worldwide Responsible Accredited Production certification confirming lawful, humane, and ethical manufacturing throughout our facilities.

  • No. 03

    OEKO-TEXStandard 100Harmful-substance tested

    Standard 100 certification guaranteeing that our products are tested for harmful substances and are safe for human and environmental health.

  • No. 04

    GRSGlobal Recycled Standard

    Global Recycled Standard certification validating recycled content claims and responsible social and environmental practices in production.

  • No. 05

    OCSOrganic Content Standard

    Organic Content Standard certification for products containing 5% or more organic material, ensuring organic fiber integrity throughout the supply chain.

  • No. 06

    BSCISocial compliance

    Business Social Compliance Initiative certification ensuring fair working conditions, ethical labor practices, and respect for human rights.

  • No. 07

    SEDEXEthical data exchange

    Supplier Ethical Data Exchange membership demonstrating ethical supply chain management and transparency in our labor, health, and environmental practices.

  • No. 08

    Better WorkILO · IFC programme

    ILO–IFC partnership programme ensuring improved working conditions and labor compliance with international standards.

  • No. 09

    Higg IndexSustainability measurement

    Sustainable Apparel Coalition Higg Facility Environmental Module, used to measure and improve our facility’s sustainability performance.

Our commitment

Beyond compliance: operational accountability.

At TSF, certifications reflect our operational commitment to auditable, responsible manufacturing, maintained through continuous monitoring and dedicated compliance teams. We treat these standards as minimum requirements that we strive to exceed.

Our compliance team continuously monitors and improves our processes, so that we maintain our certifications and lead the industry in ethical and sustainable manufacturing practices.
