01

Yarn

Combed cotton arrives on cone and is loaded to the creel. Every lot is tested for count and strength before it reaches a machine.

CONE TO CREEL
02

Knit

Circular knitting builds the loop structure that determines how the finished garment will hang, stretch and recover.

GREIGE FABRIC
03

Dye

Wet processing and finishing. Colour is matched to the buyer's standard and held across the full production run.

WET PROCESSING
04

Cut

Fabric is spread to ply and cut against the marker. Efficiency here decides how much of the roll becomes garment.

MARKER, SPREAD, PLY
05

Sew

Assembly. Panels become a garment, and the seam is where construction quality is either present or absent.

ASSEMBLY LINE
06

Pack

Inspected, folded, cartoned. Eighty thousand pieces leave this sequence every working day.
