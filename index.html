  1. Station01

    Circular knitting

    YarnJersey · rib · fleece

    Yarn is knitted into greige jersey, rib and fleece on circular knitting machines.

  2. Station02

    Dyeing

    To shadeDyed and finished before cutting

    Greige fabric is dyed and finished to shade before it reaches the cutting room.

  3. Station03

    Fabric stock + spreading

    500Tons of ready greige stock

    Knit programs start from stock. With about 500 tons of ready greige stock and 500+ tons of cotton/CVC jersey on hand, single-jersey orders can turn in approximately 30 days.

  4. Station04

    CAD cutting

    CADPrecision, multi-layer cutting

    Markers are optimized in CAD to minimize waste, then multi-layer lays are cut on automated Gerber Paragon cutters, with inspection at every stage.

  5. Station05

    Embellishment

    65,000+Pieces daily printing capacity

    Screen printing or embroidery, in-house. Six automatic screen printing machines lay down multi-color prints in water-based and plastisol inks; four computerized multi-head embroidery machines finish logos and details.

  6. Station06

    Sewing

    3,500Sewing machines

    Trained operators and automatic sewing machines across ten production units. Egypt’s largest flat-seam inventory backs our athletic, underwear and swimwear programs.

  7. Station07

    QC inspection

    AQLSampling · in-line + final

    In-line quality checks and final inspection against each customer’s specification, under an ISO 9001 quality management system.

  8. Station08

    Finishing + packing

    100,000Units daily production capacity

    Pressed, folded, labeled, tagged and poly-bagged according to client specifications, ready for export.

  9. Station09

    Cartons + warehouse

    24/7Operations capability

    Dedicated warehousing for raw materials and finished goods. Flat-pack, garment-on-hanger (GOH) or custom packing to retailer requirements.

  10. Station10

    Container + export

    Duty-freeTo the US (QIZ) and EU (Euro 1)

    Our own logistics arm, Green Point Logistics, runs customs and freight from Alexandria: about one week to most of Europe, about three weeks to major US ports.
