COMMON QUESTIONS

Everything you need to know

What is the freight lead time from Egypt to the USA and Europe?

Our strategic location in Alexandria, Egypt offers competitive transit times to major global markets. Freight to most European countries typically arrives within one week, while shipments to major U.S. ports average approximately three weeks. Our in-house logistics arm, Green Point Logistics, manages all shipping operations to ensure timely and efficient delivery.

Is Egypt duty-free to the USA and Europe?

Yes. Egypt enjoys duty-free access to the United States under the Qualifying Industrial Zones (QIZ) agreement, and duty-free access to Europe under the Euro-Mediterranean Partnership (Euro 1) agreement. These trade agreements make Egypt one of the most cost-effective manufacturing origins for brands serving North American and European markets.

What is the total order lead time from placement to delivery?

We offer a rapid turnaround of approximately 30 days for Single Jersey fabrics, as we maintain over 600 tons of greige fabric in-house for immediate production. For most locally sourced fabrics, including Jersey and Fleece for knit garments, the lead time is approximately 45 days from order confirmation to delivery.

How is your materials sourcing structured?

We locally source approximately 60% of our production requirements for basic knit fabrics through our strong domestic supplier network. Woven fabrics and specialty performance materials are sourced internationally through our dedicated fabric sourcing and quality office in China. We maintain a local stock of over 450 tons of cotton and CVC jersey to support rapid production cycles.

What types of garments does Three Stars Fashion manufacture?

We manufacture a comprehensive range of apparel categories across men's, ladies', kids', uniforms, and underwear segments. Our product range includes activewear and athletic garments (hoodies, leggings, sports bras, rash guards, soft-shell jackets), casualwear (t-shirts, polos, pants, dresses, outerwear), and uniforms and underwear (scrubs, team uniforms, boxers, nightwear). We are particularly recognized for our expertise in athletic and performance garments, supported by one of the largest flat-seam machine inventories in Egypt.

What certifications and compliance standards does TSF hold?

Three Stars Fashion operates under internationally recognized compliance, ethical, and sustainability standards. Our certifications include ISO 9001, WRAP (Gold), Better Work, BSCI, SEDEX, Higg Index, GRS (Global Recycled Standard), OCS (Organic Content Standard), OEKO-TEX Standard 100, FAMA (Disney), and Inditex Approved Facility status. We are also approved by major global brands and retailers such as Walmart, Under Armour, Fruit of the Loom, PVH, and Primark.

Do you offer in-house product development and sampling?

Yes. TSF offers comprehensive product development capabilities, supporting customers from initial concept through to bulk production. Our services include in-house design, development, and sampling, as well as customization based on your specific requirements. We also maintain a dedicated fabric sourcing and quality office in China, and have a strong supplier network for performance, cotton, recycled, and sustainable fabrics to meet diverse program needs.
