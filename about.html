OUR STORY

A legacy of excellence in Egyptian manufacturing

Three Stars Fashion Group (TSF) is a pioneering garment manufacturing company and prominent apparel exporter based in Alexandria, Egypt. Established in 2008, TSF specializes in the production of high-quality knitted and woven garments for globally recognized brands and retailers.

Driven by innovation, sustainability, and social responsibility, we combine advanced manufacturing technology with skilled craftsmanship across our state-of-the-art facilities. Our vertically integrated structure allows us to deliver consistent quality, competitive lead times, and scalable production solutions tailored to our partners' needs.

Our group of companies includes Three Stars Fashion, Three Stars Apparels, Golden Stars Textile, and Green Point Logistics. Together, we are ranked as one of the largest apparel manufacturers and exporters in Egypt.

OUR VALUES

Principles that guide
every stitch

Sustainability

Committed to reducing our environmental footprint through eco-friendly practices and the use of sustainable materials and energy. We're transitioning to 70-80% solar power by 2026.

Innovation

Constantly evolving our processes and products with cutting-edge technology and creative thinking while ensuring the highest standards in every garment, from design to delivery.

Ethical Practices

Upholding fair labor practices and contributing to the well-being of our employees and communities. We actively support local communities through food programs and healthcare partnerships.

Customer Satisfaction

Prioritizing the needs and preferences of our customers in every aspect of our business. We ensure prompt response times, reliable delivery, and transparency from sourcing to final product.

VISION 2030

To be a global leader in sustainable fashion, setting new benchmarks

To be a global leader in sustainable fashion, setting new benchmarks in environmental stewardship, innovation, and social responsibility. We are revolutionizing the way garments are produced.

Looking ahead, Three Stars Fashion Group is focused on expanded manufacturing capacity and automation, deeper integration of renewable energy, broader adoption of recycled and organic materials, expansion into new global markets, and continued investment in technology, people, and compliance.
