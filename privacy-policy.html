Privacy policy.

What information we collect, how we use it, and your rights with respect to your data.

Last updated: April 2026

Three Stars Fashion Group (“TSF”, “we”, “us”, or “our”) is committed to protecting your personal information. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your rights with respect to your data when you visit threestarsfashion.com or contact us for business inquiries.

1. Information we collect

When you use our contact or Submit an Inquiry form, we may collect the following information:

  • Contact details: your name, company name, email address, and phone number
  • Inquiry details: your message, project requirements, and any other information you voluntarily provide
  • Technical data: standard website log data including your IP address, browser type, and pages visited

We do not collect any sensitive personal data (such as payment card information) through this website.

2. How we use your information

We use the information we collect solely for legitimate business purposes, including:

  • Responding to your inquiries and requests for quotations
  • Managing and developing our business relationship with you
  • Providing information about our manufacturing services and capabilities
  • Improving our website and communications

We will not use your information for unsolicited marketing without your prior consent.

3. Data sharing

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties. We may share your information only in the following limited circumstances:

  • Group companies: your information may be shared with our affiliate companies (Three Stars Apparels, Golden Stars Textile, Green Point Logistics) as necessary to fulfill your manufacturing or logistics inquiries
  • Service providers: we use Tally.so to process contact form submissions. Their privacy policy governs how they handle your data
  • Legal compliance: we may disclose your information where required by applicable law or regulation

4. Cookies & analytics

Our website may use standard browser cookies to ensure the website functions correctly and to improve your browsing experience. We do not use cookies to track you across other websites. You can control cookie settings through your browser preferences.

5. Data retention & security

We retain your contact information for as long as necessary to fulfill the purpose for which it was collected, or as required by applicable law. We implement appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect your personal data against unauthorized access, alteration, or loss.

6. Your rights

Depending on your location, you may have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you
  • Request correction of inaccurate personal data
  • Request deletion of your personal data
  • Object to or restrict the processing of your data

To exercise any of these rights, please contact us at info@tsf.com.eg. We will respond to your request within a reasonable timeframe.

7. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices or applicable regulations. The updated date at the top of this page will reflect any revisions. We encourage you to review this policy periodically.

8. Contact us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or how we handle your personal data, please contact us:
